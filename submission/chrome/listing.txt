LANGUAGE
English

CATEGORY
Productivity / Workflow & Planning (choose the closest current dashboard category).

SUMMARY (manifest description)
Save and close tabs, search a private local archive, organise useful links and export them as Markdown.

DESCRIPTION
Too many tabs open because you might need them later? Tab Dumper gives them a home.

• Save tabs before closing them.
• Search saved titles, links, descriptions and available page text.
• Organise links with categories and purposes such as Read later, To do or Reference.
• Revisit up to ten links at a time with an optional weekly reminder.
• Export JSON backups or Markdown files for Obsidian and Notion.

Your archive stays on your device. No account, tracking, advertising, cloud sync or AI service. Saved links stay until you delete them.

Some pages can only be saved as links. Captures contain plain text, not a complete offline copy of the website. Pinned tabs, playing audio and detected unfinished edits are left open, but always save your work before closing tabs.

The optional weekly reminder checks its schedule in the background and displays a toolbar dot and dashboard banner. It never automatically closes tabs or deletes entries.

Export a backup before uninstalling or changing browser profiles. Installing from another store or changing extension IDs creates a separate archive; use Export backup and Import backup to transfer your saved links.

TEST INSTRUCTIONS
No account, credentials or payment is needed. Load the extension, open two ordinary web pages, then click its toolbar icon. Assign a category and Save & close one tab. Search the archived entry, open Preview & edit, change its description, and reopen it. Export a JSON backup and import the same backup: entries should merge. Export Markdown. Open Weekly review and Keep an entry. Try saving a pinned tab or a page with an edited form: it should stay open. Help & privacy is available in the sidebar. Text capture may be unavailable on restricted pages, including browser/store pages; those can be link-only or excluded. The archive and saved-text previews work offline.
