# Tab Dumper Privacy Policy

Updated: 26 October 2026 · Developer: Federico Viscioletti

## Data accessed and saved

Tab Dumper reads open-tab titles and URLs to display your tabs. When you explicitly preview, save or unload a page, it inspects that page for readable text and possible unsaved edits. Capture runs only following your actions, including selections of background tabs.

Saved entries can include URLs, titles, descriptions, page text, categories, purpose and save/review dates. Screenshots imported from earlier versions may also be retained. Form-field values are excluded from saved page text. Readable pages you choose to archive may contain personal or sensitive information.

Tab Dumper does not request device geolocation or independently track your location. Location information already present in a page or URL you choose to save may be included in that entry. Private-window tabs are not supported. The extension does not access the browser's browsing-history database, cookies or authentication tokens.

## Local storage and sharing

Your archive and reminder settings are stored locally in your browser profile. Tab Dumper does not transmit your archive to the developer or a third-party service. It has no analytics, advertising, cloud synchronisation or AI service. Scripts and fonts are bundled. Background activity checks the review-reminder schedule and migrates older local archives.

## Retention and your control

Archived entries remain until you delete them. You can edit entries, delete them, export JSON backups or Markdown files, and disable reminders. Deletion removes the active archive record; exported files and other copies must be deleted separately.

Supported legacy copies are removed after successful migration. Unsupported or malformed legacy records may remain until the extension is uninstalled. Uninstalling the extension or deleting your browser profile can remove your archive. Export a backup first if you want to keep it.

## Data protection

The archive uses browser-managed extension storage. Tab Dumper does not add its own encryption or password protection. Exported backups and Markdown files are not encrypted by the extension. Protect your device, browser profile and exported files, particularly when they contain private page content.

The extension limits its own network connections through its content security policy and renders captured text as text rather than executable webpage code. These protections do not replace your device's access controls.

## External websites and support

Reopening an archived link contacts its website normally, under that website's own privacy practices. The product website is separate from the extension and has its own privacy and cookie controls.

Support is available through [the Tab Dumper website](https://viscioletti.com/tab-dumper) or [the assistance form](https://tally.so/r/e55RWl). The optional form is hosted by Tally. Your email address, request and any optional details you submit are made available to Federico Viscioletti to handle your request, not for marketing. Your archive is never attached automatically. Do not submit passwords, private page content or archive backups.

Tally's service practices are described in its [privacy policy](https://tally.so/help/privacy-policy). For privacy questions or to request deletion of a support submission, select “Privacy or data request” in the assistance form.

## Limited use

Tab Dumper uses data accessed through browser APIs only to provide its tab archiving, search, review and export features. It does not sell this data, use it for advertising, creditworthiness or lending decisions, or transfer it for unrelated purposes. The developer does not receive or read your local archive. These practices follow the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
